# Search Intent Notes

## Main Principle
Article structure should reflect what the searcher wants to know first.

## For Company Analysis Queries
- What kind of business is this?
- Why does it matter for investors now?
- What are the earnings drivers?
- What are the key risks and watch points?

## Structural Implication
- Put the short answer early
- Use deeper sections for readers who continue
- Add internal links that map to next-step curiosity
