## SEO Introduction Rules

The introduction must:
1. State the conclusion first
2. Include the company name and core search intent keywords naturally
3. Explain what the reader will learn
4. Provide a simple hook before entering deep analysis
5. Help both beginner and advanced readers understand where to start

## Good Structure
- 1st paragraph: conclusion or overall judgment
- 2nd paragraph: what this article explains and why it matters now
- 3rd paragraph: short bullet summary or key takeaways
- 4th paragraph: transition into deeper analysis

## Two-Layer Guidance
- The opening should satisfy a beginner-level query quickly
- The transition should signal that deeper analysis follows
- Do not force advanced detail into the first paragraph

## Internal Link Guidance In The Intro
- Link one supporting explainer if the topic requires background knowledge
- Link one related company or sector article if comparison helps search intent
- Avoid adding too many links in the first screen

## Avoid
- Starting with abstract concepts only
- Using technical framing before answering the reader's likely question
- Overly long introductions
- Delaying the article's conclusion until the middle
