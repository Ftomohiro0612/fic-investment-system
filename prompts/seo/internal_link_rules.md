## Internal Link Rules

Internal links should guide the reader to the next most useful article.

## Link Types To Prioritize
- Supporting explainer article
- Related company analysis
- Sector or theme overview
- Follow-up article for deeper analysis

## Placement Guidance
- Up to 2 links near the top if they improve understanding
- Natural links inside the body where a concept needs support
- A short related reading section near the end

## Anchor Text Guidance
- Keep anchor text descriptive
- Avoid repeating the same anchor text every time
- Match the reader's likely next question
