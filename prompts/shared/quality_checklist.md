## Quality Checklist

- Does the introduction state the conclusion early?
- Is the article aligned with the target query?
- Is there a useful summary block near the top?
- Is the first layer understandable for non-expert readers?
- Does the deeper layer add genuine analytical value?
- Are internal links helpful and intentional?
- For every related company/article, can the writer explain the concrete connection in one sentence using a driver such as same customer, supply chain, direct competitor, substitute technology, shared regulation/policy, shared upstream variable, or direct financing/capital-market exposure?
- Are any related links included only because the target is a large company, same broad sector, recently published, or SEO-friendly? If yes, remove them.
- Are risks and watch points clearly stated?
