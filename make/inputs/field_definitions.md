# Field Definitions

## Main Inputs
- `company_name`: official company name
- `stock_code`: stock code or ticker
- `article_theme`: main article angle
- `integrated_memo`: consolidated research memo
- `key_drivers`: main business and earnings drivers
- `risk_factors`: main downside factors
- `watch_points`: indicators to monitor
- `related_articles`: existing relevant internal articles

## Recommended Additional Inputs
- `target_query`: primary search query or search intent
- `reader_level`: target emphasis such as beginner, mixed, or advanced
- `sector_context`: sector or peer context needed for comparison

## Notes
- `integrated_memo` is the main source for the article body
- `related_articles` should be used for internal link suggestions
- `article_theme` should guide the framing of the introduction
- `target_query` should influence the title, intro, and early section order
- `reader_level` helps calibrate the two-layer structure
